# Goal C.1 — Visual review

The following PDFs were rendered with Poppler (`pdftoppm`) and inspected as
page images: C-A, C-D, C-E, C-F and the C-MULTI E2E.

## Findings

- **C-A:** the immediate action, annual benefit and intervention cost are
  visible on the first page; the card is compact and readable.
- **C-D:** the negative decision label, economic benefit and production/freshness
  blocker appear together. It does not resemble a hidden rejection.
- **C-E:** the no-finding result is a compact one-page document. It contains
  conclusion, checks, limits and sources without invented action or filler
  page.
- **C-F:** repair and replacement remain non-cumulative and show benefit, cost,
  return, planned downtime, operational burden and uncertainty.
- **C-MULTI:** ACT_NOW, INVESTIGATE_FIRST, MONITOR and the negative operational
  decision all remain visually distinct within one report.

## Layout checks

- no clipped text or PDF overflow observed;
- no redundant final summary page;
- headers remain adjacent to their first content block;
- monetary values and decision labels are easy to locate;
- the chart has visible title, axes, legend and activity context in the PDF;
- compact reports can have whitespace, but no page exists solely to fill a
  target count.

Rendered page PNGs are included in the Goal C.1 review bundle.

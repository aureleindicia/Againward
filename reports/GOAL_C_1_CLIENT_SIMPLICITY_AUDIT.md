# Goal C.1 — Client simplicity audit

- Can a small-business owner identify the one to three items needing attention
  in minutes? **YES.** The first page shows counters and at most three concise
  priorities.
- Can the owner understand why a possible saving is not recommended? **YES.**
  a negative decision card shows the potential benefit and the sourced
  operational blocker together.
- Does each page contain useful information? **YES.** The redundant “À retenir”
  page was removed; the renderer only allocates overflow pages.
- Does the report feel visually empty or artificially stretched? **NO.** A
  true no-finding may be a compact one-page report instead of filler pages.
- Does it feel unnecessarily dense or technical? **NO.** Internal identifiers,
  provenance chains and enum names remain out of the client PDF.
- Can an owner understand important charts without an energy engineer? **YES.**
  each chart block displays title, the decision question, axes, legend and an
  activity marker when that context is used.

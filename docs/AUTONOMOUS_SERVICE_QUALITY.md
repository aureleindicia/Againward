# AGAINWARD internal autonomy and quality contract

Status: **engineering objective, not a verified performance claim**. This
clarification supersedes any older playbook wording that asks the founder to
manually reconstruct a normal investigation or approve every native-text fact.
The externally sold offer remains an asynchronous B2B analysis/advisory service,
not access to an AI tool or a promise to replace a client's employees.

## Production responsibility

The internal analyst reads the authorized source batch, classifies documents,
proposes facts, resolves source-backed relationships, investigates apparent
discrepancies and counter-explanations, requests high-value clarification only
when needed, calculates with the deterministic Rental engine, writes the
client report and performs an independent adversarial check. Source hashes,
privacy versions, exact quotes or visual locations, review decisions, ledger
formulas and report hashes remain replayable. A model cannot grant privacy
clearance, impersonate a person or authorize delivery.

The founder's routine work is a short final review of the finished report and
delivery authorization. Clear visual facts can pass through independent
multimodal reread, pixel-bound adjudication when disputed, and a distinct MODEL
fact receipt. A human checks original pixels only when the evidence or an
applicable authority requirement remains genuinely unresolved. Material unresolved
contract priority, identity, net/gross basis or evidence gaps trigger a concise
exception/clarification; they are not converted into guessed claims. This is
an exception path, not a standing requirement to approve hundreds of facts.

Current technical state remains below verified quality readiness: one known
eight-source synthetic Rental dossier has autonomously reached a source-backed,
model-QA-checked evaluation PDF, including a scanned return. A script-created
`HUMAN` record is never evidence of the owner's review. The second pass uses
the same model family and can share errors; it is an omission/disagreement
screen, not independent outcome truth. Report correctness and routine human
review burden remain unmeasured on a blind cohort.

## Quantitative objectives and denominators

Target at least **99% material correctness of delivered reports** and at least
**95% of supported cases with no human correction and zero human report-writing**.
Neither has been measured or achieved. A report is materially correct only if
every positive financial claim has the correct source/entity/term/period/credit
and amount, no material supported discrepancy was missed, and limitations and
abstentions are honest. Count one report as failed if any material condition
fails. Count all admitted supported cases in the autonomy denominator,
including cases escalated or abstained after intake; separately publish delivered
coverage and the share of supported cases deferred. Report precision without
coverage is not enough.

For each frozen evaluation split record:

| Measure | Required observation |
|---|---|
| End-to-end correctness | Correct reports / all delivered reports; exact independent adjudication of every material claim and omission. |
| Critical harm | Wrong financial amount, unsupported positive, missed material discrepancy, double count, currency conflation, forged/missing provenance. |
| Extraction and resolution | Field/value/role/line exactness, entity-link precision/recall, source-component coverage, scan and native separately. |
| Adversarial QA | Primary errors caught, residual errors after reconciliation, new false disputes, and time/cost added. |
| Autonomy | Supported cases needing no human correction, human-written report count (target zero), exceptions per case, final-review minutes. |
| Throughput | Receipt-to-report wall time, model time/usage and monetary cost where available, CPU/memory, per-case processing cost. |
| Coverage | Supported/admitted/delivered/abstained counts, reasons, no-result reports and client clarification rate. |

Use independent blind source-to-report cases with varied vendors, layouts,
languages and adversarial documents. Freeze code, prompt, thresholds and
supported scope before a holdout. Keep oracle and generator unavailable to
production participants. A same-agent, known-truth synthetic run is DEV evidence,
not blind validation. Human correction/time is measured from actual operator
activity, never inferred from a JSON role. Report confidence intervals or
sample-size limitations; do not claim 99% from a handful of perfect examples.
The pilot guardrail remains zero **known** unsupported positive claims in its
release-blocking challenge, not a promise of zero real-world risk. If QA misses
an error, correct and rerun a fresh held-out challenge rather than tuning on
the holdout.

## Simple asynchronous client journey

Written scope and proposal → explicit processing permission and secure upload
instructions → bounded document intake → short written clarification only for
decision-changing gaps → internal investigation and QA → finished documented
PDF plus practical actions → founder's brief final review and delivery
authorization → secure asynchronous delivery and corrections channel. No cold
call or routine meeting is required by the operating model. The client must
be told about relevant AI/provider processing, confidentiality and retention
under the actual approved contract/configuration; this document is not a legal
or provider-compliance certification.

Release remains **NOT READY** until a source-to-report, model-driven challenge,
independent blind assessment, genuine operator visual/final review, final CI,
and owner-controlled business/privacy/provider requirements are recorded.

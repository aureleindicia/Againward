# Final release boundaries

The service is an asynchronous B2B Rental investigation and advisory report,
not a SaaS interface, guaranteed recovery product or legal ruling.

## Technical boundaries

- Raw processing requires existing contract/provider authority. The new model
  privacy reader cannot grant it. Native PDF format and ordinary professional
  contacts are not blanket exclusions.
- Secrets, medical/HR-sensitive content, unnecessary identity documents and
  genuinely uninspectable components do not pass to business analysis.
- Visual originals require current source/candidate/render-bound inspection.
  Independently reread and, when disputed, source-pixel-adjudicated visual facts
  may receive `MODEL` receipts for analysis; unresolved pixels still escalate
  to HUMAN/required information. MODEL/scripted receipts are never HUMAN,
  deterministic semantic proof or delivery approval.
- Model reads, investigates and writes; deterministic code owns quantities,
  periods, amounts, allocation, rounding and totals. A documentary difference
  does not establish its cause, recoverability or legal entitlement.
- Native/visual QA remains bounded by source context, visual-page and evidence
  budgets. Ambiguous links or unsupported contractual semantics require a
  precise clarification/STOP, not guessed normalization.
- Source/privacy/policy revisions invalidate dependent work and archive earlier
  analysis, including approvals. Historical absolute references are historical
  audit records, not current executable package references.
- Final delivery remains owner authorized against exact current artifacts.
  Neither `READY_FOR_APPROVAL` nor an evaluation QA pass is delivery permission.

## Owner-controlled, not engineering approvals

Actual client processing rights, provider restrictions, legal/commercial terms,
retention choices, genuine exceptional visual review and final delivery approval.
No real client documents have been authorized by synthetic benchmark contracts.

## Evidence boundary

99% correctness and 95% correction-free cases remain engineering targets.
Same-model source/report QA, known synthetic DEV dossiers and passing automated
tests cannot establish those rates. Do not market them as measured performance.
